IT AND ASSET USAGE POLICY

Every employee is issued one company laptop and one email account on
joining. Laptops must be returned to the IT desk within 3 working days of
resignation or termination.

Passwords for company systems must be at least 12 characters long, include
a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers and symbols, and must be changed
every 90 days. Reusing the last 5 passwords is not allowed.

Employees must enable disk encryption and endpoint antivirus on all company
devices. Installing unauthorized software or connecting personal USB drives
to company laptops is prohibited without IT approval.

Any lost or stolen device must be reported to the IT security team within
1 hour of discovery so the device can be remotely wiped. VPN access is
mandatory for connecting to internal systems while working outside the
office network.
