LEAVE POLICY

All full-time employees are entitled to 18 casual/annual leaves per calendar
year, credited at 1.5 leaves per month. Casual leave cannot be carried
forward beyond December 31st; a maximum of 5 unused leaves may be carried
into the next year with manager approval.

Sick leave is provided separately at 10 days per year and does not require
advance notice, but a doctor's note is required for absences longer than
2 consecutive days.

Employees must apply for planned leave at least 3 working days in advance
through the HR portal. Leave requests are approved by the reporting manager.
Unplanned leave (e.g. emergencies) should be informed to the manager as soon
as possible, ideally before the start of the working day.

Maternity leave is 26 weeks as per company policy, and paternity leave is
2 weeks, both fully paid. Leave without pay (LWP) may be granted at the
discretion of HR for exceptional circumstances beyond the standard leave
balance.
