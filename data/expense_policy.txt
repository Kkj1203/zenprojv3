EXPENSE REIMBURSEMENT POLICY

Employees travelling for official work may claim reimbursement for travel,
accommodation, and meals. Domestic flight bookings must be made at least
7 days in advance and require manager pre-approval for fares above INR
15,000.

Meal expenses during business travel are capped at INR 1,200 per day for
domestic trips and USD 60 per day for international trips. Original
receipts must be uploaded to the expense portal within 15 days of the
expense being incurred; claims submitted after 30 days will not be
processed.

Local conveyance (cabs, auto, metro) for official meetings can be claimed
up to INR 500 per day without a receipt, and any amount above that requires
a valid bill. Reimbursements are processed with the next payroll cycle
after approval by the reporting manager and finance team.
